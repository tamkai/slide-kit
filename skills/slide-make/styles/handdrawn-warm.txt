## BACKGROUND (important adjustment)
- Background is BRIGHT WHITE base (#FFFFFF to a very light off-white #FCFCFA). Airy, light, lots
  of white space. NOT cream, NOT beige — keep it crisp and bright.
- Optionally a few very subtle light-gray or pale-teal hand-drawn texture marks in corners, but
  the dominant feel is clean white and open.

## ILLUSTRATION
- Hand-drawn line illustration style: warm, slightly loose ink/pen linework with a friendly,
  editorial magazine-illustration quality. Human and inviting, with confident loose linework
  that has VARYING WEIGHT (some strokes thicker, some thin) — not uniform thin outlines.

### People (important — taller proportions)
- Draw people with REALISTIC, TALLER proportions: about 5–6 heads tall, NOT chibi / NOT
  big-head cartoon. Natural body proportions, like a warm grown-up editorial illustration.
- Recurring family cast (keep consistent across slides):
  Mom — teal/turquoise top; Son — yellow hoodie; Dad — black/charcoal clothes.
  Friendly, relaxed, everyday expressions and poses (thinking, trying, talking, relaxing).
- Warm hand-drawn line art with flat color fills inside the lines.

### Mascot (hand-drawn "atelier morning" flavor, not a generic robot)
- When a mascot is helpful, draw a SOFT, hand-drawn SAGE-GREEN character — gentle, rounded,
  matte, with the warm "atelier morning" hand-touch (calm, organic, a little clay-like in
  feeling but still flat hand-drawn line art). Sage body (#8B9A7D), with subtle rose/gold
  touches. NOT a shiny metallic generic robot, NOT cyber, NOT chrome. Quiet and friendly.

### Other subjects
- Everyday objects (notebooks, devices, coffee, tools), screens, simple props — warm hand-drawn
  line art with flat color fills. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not isometric.

## COLOR PALETTE (accents on white — "atelier morning")
- Base: BRIGHT WHITE / near-white (keep it crisp and light — NOT cream, NOT beige).
- Accent colors (flat fills and hand-drawn marks on the white base). Blend the friendly design

  Teal / turquoise (#2BB7B7) and SAGE green (#8B9A7D) as primary accents,
  warm gold (#C4A057) and soft yellow (#F4C84A),
  dusty rose / salmon (#E8A598) and a sparing coral/red (#E8604C) for a single pop,
  with charcoal ink (#2C3330) for linework and text.
- "色は道具のように点在する": colors appear in SMALL areas as accents; the bright white base

- Flat colors, no heavy gradients.
