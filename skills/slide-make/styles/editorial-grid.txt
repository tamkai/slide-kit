## Visual Style: Editorial Grid — sophisticated muted, commercial-studio, seminar-grade

A World-Class Editorial Designer's slide. A clean 2x2 grid composition with bold geometric
overlays, the subject physically embedded into the grid (sandwich layering), a sophisticated
muted palette, and matte flat color blocks. This is a SLIDE for a live seminar — it must read
instantly and carry a short Japanese title + a few keyword tags, while staying gallery-grade.

## PHASE 1 — SUBJECT & SANDWICH LAYERING
- The "subject" of each slide is the central object/metaphor described in the per-slide brief
  (a device, a person, a prop, an icon — whatever the brief names). Render it as high-end
  commercial studio photography / a clean dimensional object.
- Sandwich Effect (create real depth): interweave the subject with the background geometric
  blocks. Some parts of the subject must be HIDDEN BEHIND geometric blocks, while other parts
  (edges, limbs, props, wheels) OVERLAP and sit in FRONT of them. This front/behind interplay
  is essential.

## PHASE 2 — GRID & GEOMETRY
- Layout: a clean 2x2 grid composition dividing the canvas into four quadrants.
- Overlays: superimpose large, bold geometric ARCS and CIRCLES over the grid (flat, confident,
  oversized). They cross quadrant boundaries.
- Visual Balance: place one iconic prop (from the brief's labels/metaphor) FLOATING in a
  separate quadrant to balance the main subject.

## PHASE 3 — SOPHISTICATED MUTED PALETTE
- DO NOT use aggressive neon or oversaturated colors.
- Take the natural colors implied by the subject and shift them to a "Sophisticated Muted"
  spectrum: desaturated, earthy, dusty versions (dusty rose instead of hot pink, sage green
  instead of bright mint, slate blue instead of royal blue, warm sand/clay neutrals).
- Keep a consistent muted palette across ALL slides in the deck so they feel like one set.
- Finish: MATTE, FLAT color blocks. ZERO gradients.

## PHASE 4 — PHOTOGRAPHY & LIGHTING
- Subject style: high-end commercial studio photography quality.
- Lighting: soft, diffused studio lighting with gentle highlights. No harsh shadows.
- Integration: the subject must feel physically embedded into the graphic grid (consistent with
  the sandwich layering of Phase 1).

## PHASE 5 — BRANDING & TEXT (seminar adaptation)
- Reserve ONE background grid block as a "branding/title block": place the short Japanese TITLE
  there cleanly (single weight, no tagline). If a logo/symbol is provided in the brief, place it
  as a clean single-color iconic symbol — otherwise just the title text.
- Keyword tags: render the short Japanese keywords from the brief as small, clean labels placed
  near the relevant object or within empty quadrants. Minimal, editorial — not stickers.
- TEXT RULE: render only the title and the specified keyword tags. No paragraphs, no taglines,
  no invented words. Japanese renders reliably; keep it short and exact. Let the grid + subject
  carry the meaning.

## CROSS-SLIDE CONSISTENCY
- Same muted palette, same matte-flat finish, same 2x2 grid logic, same arc/circle overlay
  language, same studio-light quality, same title-block treatment on every slide of the deck.

## Layout notes
- ONE clear focal subject per slide. Generous negative space inside quadrants.
- Bold, calm, premium editorial magazine feel. Not busy, not neon, not cartoonish.
